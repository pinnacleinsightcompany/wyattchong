Overview

Experience built across industries, not inside a single box.

Wyatt Chong began his professional journey in banking in 1992. Since then, his career has moved through office technology, pharmaceuticals, industrial equipment, regional sales, business management and entrepreneurship.

That breadth shaped a practical way of thinking about business: understand the market, understand people, build trust, adapt quickly and create value.

Today, Wyatt combines decades of commercial experience with a new focus on entrepreneurship, artificial intelligence and professional development through Pinnacle Insight Company.
